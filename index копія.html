Privacy Policy — Night Stadium: Soccer Manager

Last updated: 21 October 2026. Developer: MP Games.

Night Stadium is a free offline game.

Data stored on your device. Game saves and settings are stored only on your device. We have no servers and do not receive this data.

Advertising. The game shows rewarded videos only when you tap the corresponding button yourself. Ads are served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location based on IP address, ad interaction data and diagnostics, in accordance with Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases. Purchases are processed by Google Play / Apple App Store. RevenueCat is used to verify purchases (anonymous user ID, purchase history): https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. We never see your payment details.

Children. The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
